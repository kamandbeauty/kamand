## Full description

```
ROOZI is a Persian daily planner: simple and light.

Write down your tasks in seconds, see them on the Jalali calendar, and get
reminded on time. No sign-up required.


■ A real Jalali calendar

The whole app is built on the Hijri Shamsi calendar rather than a translated
Gregorian one. Leap years, month lengths and weekdays are all calculated
correctly, and numbers are rendered with Persian digits.


■ Daily tasks

• Quick add straight from the Today screen
• Categories, priorities and notes per task
• Undated tasks, for things you have not scheduled yet
• Drag and drop to reorder
• Swipe right to complete, left to delete
• Search across everything


■ Repeating tasks

Daily, weekly on chosen days, or monthly on the Jalali calendar. Set it once
and forget it.


■ Exact reminders

Reminders are scheduled as exact alarms and arrive even when the app is
closed. Snooze and Done sit on the notification itself, so you never have to
open the app.


■ Notes and notebooks

Organise notes into separate notebooks and pin the important ones to the top.


■ Birthday notebook

Record the birthdays that matter. ROOZI counts the days and ages on the Jalali
calendar, reminds you in advance, and includes 50 ready greetings personalised
with the person's name. Gift ideas can be saved and turned into tasks.


■ Progress and statistics

Completion rate, done and pending counts, a weekly performance chart, and a
streak counter for the days you kept your plan.


■ Home screen widgets

See today's tasks without opening the app, or add a new one in a single tap.


■ Make it yours

Six colour themes, light and dark mode, and full Persian and English support.
The Vazirmatn font ships with the app, so it never depends on system fonts.


■ Backup

Export everything to a single file and restore it whenever you want. The file
stays on your device.


■ Your data stays on your device

Tasks, notes and birthdays are all stored in your phone's own storage. You do
not need to create an account or hand over any details to use the app.


Android 7 and above • about 2.5 MB

Designed by Javid Studio
```
